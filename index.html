Heraldic Code

The Nine Principles of the Kempie Crest

Rooted in our official emblem, these nine foundational virtues guide character formation, classroom conduct, and sportsmanship across all phases.

I

Creativity

Original thought, inventive problem solving, and artistic courage.

II

Love

Unconditional empathy, warmth, and fellowship toward every peer.

III

Sacrifice

Dedication to team, diligence in study, and prioritizing communal good.

IV

Team Work

Cooperative learning, mutual reliance, and united achievement.

V

Respect

Reverence for educators, institutional discipline, and self-esteem.

VI

Joy

A radiant classroom atmosphere celebrating the discovery of knowledge.

VII

Kindness

Gentle daily courtesies, neurodivergent inclusion, and anti-bullying.

VIII

Charity

Active civic care demonstrated through our daily feeding scheme.

IX

Gratitude

Appreciation for educational opportunities and parental sacrifices.

Ecological Leadership & Welfare

Cultivating Sustainability, Feeding Potential.

Our on-site aquaponics facility recirculates nutrient-rich water between live tilapia tanks and organic hydroponic beds, serving as an experiential STEM laboratory while yielding fresh greens for our campus feeding scheme.

Self-Funded Daily Feeding Scheme

Ensuring approximately 50 vulnerable learners receive wholesome breakfast and lunch meals daily with absolute confidentiality and respect.
