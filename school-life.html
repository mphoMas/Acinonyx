Academic Rigour

CAPS Curriculum Across Three Dedicated Phases

Delivering national CAPS standards with 4 classes per grade (2 classes in Grade R) ensuring individual care, structured phonics, and conceptual depth.

Grade R – Grade 3

Foundation Phase

Led by Department Head Ms. T. Van Heerden. Establishing English literacy, foundational numeracy, emotional life skills, and motor stamina through structured phonics.

  • ✔ Dedicated Grade R play quad & motor stimulation
  • ✔ Intensive English vocabulary & guided reading
  • ✔ Early diagnostic screening for therapy needs
Grade 4 – Grade 6

Intermediate Phase

Led by Department Head Ms. K. Delport. Transitioning into departmentalized subject specialization, scientific inquiry, intermediate mathematics, and geography.

  • ✔ Subject specialist educators per discipline
  • ✔ Hands-on Natural Science & Technology labs
  • ✔ Formal mid-year & year-end exam preparation
Grade 7

Senior Phase

Led by Department Head Ms. J. O'Kelly. Preparing senior learners for high school excellence with Economic Management Sciences (EMS), creative arts, and student council leadership.

  • ✔ Student Prefect Council leadership induction
  • ✔ EMS entrepreneurship market day
  • ✔ High school admissions advisory & valedictory
