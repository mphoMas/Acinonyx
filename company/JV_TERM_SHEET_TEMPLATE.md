## 2. Intellectual Property (IP) Allocation & Ring-Fencing (Schedule A)

### 2.1 Acinonyx Background IP (Exclusively Retained)
All pre-existing software, frameworks, algorithms, and designs owned by Acinonyx prior to the execution date remain the sole and exclusive property of Acinonyx Consulting Group (Pty) Ltd. This includes without limitation:
- The **MAS-Core** asynchronous event bus, micro-kernel, and Finite State Machine (FSM) state space engines.
- The **Anti-Sycophancy Multi-Agent Debate (MAD)** consensus algorithms, contrarian injection protocols, and round-capping mechanics ($K \le 3$).
- The **Cognitive Memory & Comms Vault** SQLite WAL-mode FTS5 semantic telemetry schemas (`comms_vault.db`).
- The **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** JSON-RPC 2.0 client/server matrix and tool execution sandboxes.
- The **Liquid Strike Pod** dynamic squad assembly, Merkle provenance verification, and auto-disbandment engines.

**Covenant:** Under no circumstances shall the Joint Venture entity or Partner acquire any right, title, or interest in Acinonyx Background IP, other than the limited commercial license granted below.

### 2.2 Limited Commercial License to the Joint Venture
Acinonyx grants the Joint Venture a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable, royalty-[bearing / free] commercial license solely to execute runtime derivatives of the Background IP within the agreed **[Target Industry Vertical / Geographic Territory]** during the term of this agreement.

### 2.3 Foreground Intellectual Property (Jointly Developed)
All bespoke industry workflows, domain ontologies, custom user interfaces, and fine-tuned model weights derived exclusively from Partner's proprietary data during the term of the Joint Venture ("**Foreground IP**") shall be owned as follows:
- **Ownership:** Jointly owned by the Joint Venture entity, subject to Acinonyx's underlying Background IP rights.
- **Cross-Licensing:** Both Parties receive a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to utilize Foreground IP within their respective non-competing operations upon dissolution.

## 4. Governance, Steering Committee & Key-Person Continuity

### 4.1 Executive Steering Committee
The Joint Venture shall be governed by an Executive Steering Committee comprising equal representation:
- **Two (2) representatives appointed by Acinonyx** (including **Mpho Mashile** as Chief Technical Authority).
- **Two (2) representatives appointed by Partner** (including Commercial / Managing Director).
All major strategic actions (annual budgets, external licensing, executive appointments) require unanimous consent.

### 4.2 Sole Technical Authority
All architectural specifications, invariant enforcements, security approvals, and runtime deployments of the MAS-Core engine and Google Cloud infrastructure remain within the exclusive decision-making authority of Acinonyx.

### 4.3 Tripartite Source Code & Neural Architecture Escrow
To mitigate key-person and institutional continuity risks:
- The Parties agree to deposit current builds of the MAS-Core runtime engine, deployment scripts, and CI/CD pipelines with an independent, third-party software escrow agent.
- **Escrow Release Conditions:** Escrow materials shall be released to the JV entity solely upon: (i) formal liquidation or insolvency of Acinonyx; or (ii) documented permanent incapacity of key engineering personnel without designated successor.
- **Scope of Release:** Release grants the JV a perpetual operational runtime license to maintain existing deployments, without granting rights to sub-license or commercialize the raw Background IP outside the JV scope.

## 7. Autonomous Execution, Risk Allocation & Liability Caps

### 7.1 Stochastic Drift & Model Disclaimer
The Parties acknowledge that underlying third-party foundation models (e.g., Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI) operate probabilistically. Neither Acinonyx nor the Joint Venture entity warrants zero-variance outputs from external APIs. Acinonyx's sole warranty is that MAS-Core enforces deterministic FSM boundary transitions and debate verification protocols as specified in Schedule A.

### 7.2 Operational Liability Cap
**To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, liability for downstream operational transactions, erroneous automated dispatch, or financial losses executed via verified agent state transitions shall be contractually capped at twelve (12) months of aggregate platform licensing fees paid by the affected client, expressly excluding indirect, punitive, or consequential damages.**

### 7.3 Mutual Indemnification
Each Party agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other Party against third-party claims arising from: (i) gross negligence or willful misconduct; or (ii) intellectual property infringement resulting from its respective Background IP.
