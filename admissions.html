Step-by-Step Enrolment

The Gauteng Department of Education (GDE) Process

Applications for Grade 1 must be submitted via the GDE online portal, while Grade R applications are processed directly via our campus admissions office.

Step 1

Online Registration

Visit gdeadmissions.gov.za during the open GDE window and select Laerskool Kempton Park.

Step 2

Prepare Documents

Gather certified copies required by GDE and our interactive checklist within 7 days of online submission.

Step 3

Physical Submission

Submit certified physical copies at our office to Ms. P. Moyaha (Admissions Lead, ext 105).

Step 4

Placement & Welcome

Accept placement offer via SMS / online portal, and collect your official stationery list and uniform pack.

Grade R Direct Intake 2026

Grade R Applications Direct Intake

Unlike Grades 1–7, Grade R applications are processed directly by Laerskool Kempton Park. Intake is limited to 2 dedicated classes to ensure optimal teacher-to-child sensory stimulation and school readiness.

Important Notice: Applications close on Friday, 9 October 2026. Deliver completed documentation to Ms. P. Moyaha before 14:00.

Fee Exemptions & Subsidies

In strict compliance with Section 39 of the South African Schools Act (SASA), parents who cannot afford full tuition may apply for partial or 100% full fee exemptions based on verifiable combined household income.

  • ✔ Managed directly by GDE Subsidies Officers: Ms. N. Zikhali & Ms. H. Ash.
  • ✔ Exemption renewal packs must be submitted annually by 28 February.

Learner Code of Conduct Summary

Adopted under the South African Schools Act (Act 84 of 1996), our Code of Conduct establishes an orderly, disciplined, and safe haven environment where educators can teach and every child can achieve.

1. Respect & Dignity:

Zero tolerance for bullying, harassment, discrimination, or physical intimidation. Every learner and staff member is treated with mutual reverence.

2. Academic Diligence:

Punctual daily attendance, completion of homework files, and adherence to classroom decorum.

3. Campus Safety:

Care for institutional facilities, orderly playground interaction, and strict adherence to pick-up and drop-off safety gates.

The full statutory 24-page Code of Conduct document is available for inspection at the front desk and provided to every family upon enrolment placement.

Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

Under POPIA Sections 34 and 35, the processing and publication of personal information concerning minor children is strictly governed by affirmative, informed parental consent. Photography or records requests are sanitized of EXIF location data.

Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA)

Formal record requests under Section 14 may be submitted via Form 2 (available from the Information Officer) accompanied by the statutory request fee prescribed by the Information Regulator.
