Clinical Integration

The Four Pillars of Clinical Care

Delivered on campus during official school hours by licensed medical and developmental specialists.

Clinical Pillar I

Occupational Therapy (OT)

Our dedicated OT suite provides comprehensive sensory integration, addressing bilateral coordination, visual perception, pencil grip ergonomics, and emotional regulation. Sessions are scheduled during non-core periods to safeguard classroom learning.

Key Focus: Handwriting stamina, spatial orientation, executive planning, and sensory modulation.
Clinical Pillar II

Speech-Language Therapy

Led by qualified speech therapists, this wing diagnoses and rehabilitates phonological processing delays, expressive language barriers, stuttering, and auditory memory challenges, accelerating reading fluency and self-expression.

Key Focus: Articulation, narrative formulation, phonics mastery, and classroom verbal confidence.
Clinical Pillar III

On-Site Medical Clinic (Sr. B. Howe)

A full-time registered medical sister operates our on-site health suite. The clinic manages daily prescription distribution (e.g., ADHD maintenance medication), annual hearing and vision screenings, and immediate emergency trauma care.

Key Focus: Health observation, emergency triage, confidential medication administration, and immunization audits.
Clinical Pillar IV

Academic Remediation & Concessions

In partnership with certified remedial specialists and Kip McGrath Education Centres, learners experiencing foundational gaps receive small-group cognitive intervention. We facilitate formal GDE concession applications for scribes and extra time.

Key Focus: Mathematics remediation, English reading recovery, Individual Support Plans (ISPs), and examination scribes.
Institutional Leadership

The 4-Step Referral Pathway

1
Diagnostic Identification:

Educator or parent identifies a persistent learning, motor, or speech barrier.

2
SBST Assessment & ISP Formulation:

Ms. Opperman convenes with parents to review workbooks and draft an Individual Support Plan.

3
Clinical Therapy Integration:

Learner begins on-site OT or Speech therapy sessions integrated into the weekly timetable.

4
Statutory GDE Concession Lodging:

Formal lodging of medical reports with the GDE District office for exam scribes and time allowances.
