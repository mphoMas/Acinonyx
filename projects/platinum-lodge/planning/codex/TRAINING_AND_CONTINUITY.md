# Staff training and continuity draft (L2)

Review this draft with reception, finance, housekeeping, the operations supervisor, accountant and Antigravity before use. No operational policy or recovery procedure is accepted by this document alone.

Training uses synthetic guests and a non-production payment environment. Begin every exercise by checking property, role and business day. Teach routine completion and exception handling together; each participant demonstrates safe completion rather than only watching a walkthrough.

| Role | Practice | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|
| Reception | Find arrivals, verify dates/readiness, allocate, check in, amend and handle conflicts | Correct property/guest/room; authoritative reservation and readiness status |
| Finance | Review folio, identify deposit vs paid amount, reconcile pending/unknown payments, checkout corrections | Server/provider evidence, balanced ledger, authorized adjustment and fiscal document |
| Housekeeping | Find assignments, start/complete cleaning, request inspection, handle reassignment/offline state | Accepted update; supervisor approval before availability |
| Manager | Triage overdue work, verify freshness, review property boundaries and daily close | Named owner/action and agreed KPI/business-day definitions |

For each exercise include permission denial, timeout, conflicting update and wrong-property detection. Staff must explain when to stop and escalate. Record anonymous success/errors, time and support questions in the measurement sheet. Require staff and supervisor review before pilot sign-off.

During an outage, nominate an incident coordinator and record start time, affected property and approved operating restrictions. Use a restricted-access, preapproved numbered continuity log; minimize personal data and store it securely. Verify available inventory manually against the last reliable record and coordinated reception/housekeeping updates; do not promise unverified rooms or override inspection restrictions. Capture necessary payment references through the approved provider process; never record card numbers or retry uncertain payments blindly. Mark unverified financial outcomes for reconciliation.

Recovery requires the backend's approved restore/synchronization plan, reconciliation against the authoritative system, duplicate/conflict review and two-person checking of monetary differences. Do not mass-import an outage spreadsheet or silently overwrite records. Record what was accepted/rejected and why, resolve outstanding discrepancies and securely retire temporary logs according to retention policy. Backup success is not proof of restore readiness: rehearse restore and outage operation with real responsible people in a safe environment.

Before go-live: establish support contacts/escalation hours, device/access setup, property role roster, payment/provider contacts, fiscal requirements, incident communication, approved continuity template, training sign-offs and two-property pilot schedule. These are release prerequisites, not claimed outcomes.
