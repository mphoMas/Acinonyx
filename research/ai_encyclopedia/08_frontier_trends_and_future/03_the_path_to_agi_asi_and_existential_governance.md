### 1.1 Nick Bostrom’s Superintelligence Taxonomy (*Superintelligence*, 2014)
When AI surpasses human performance, **Artificial Superintelligence (ASI)** will manifest in three distinct forms:
1. **Speed Superintelligence**: A system that is qualitatively equal to a human intellect, but operates at silicon speeds (e.g., $10,000\times$ faster, reading an entire library in minutes or conducting a year of engineering research over an afternoon).
2. **Collective Superintelligence**: A decentralized swarm of millions of specialized agents collaborating across unified protocols (A2A, MCP), solving problems that no individual entity could grasp.
3. **Quality Superintelligence**: An intellect possessing cognitive modalities that human brains cannot comprehend, just as chimpanzees cannot comprehend calculus or macroeconomic trade.

### 4.1 The European Union AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689)
The world's first comprehensive horizontal legal framework for AI:
1. **Unacceptable Risk (Banned)**: Real-time public facial recognition, cognitive behavioral manipulation, biometric categorization of sensitive traits, and social scoring.
2. **High-Risk Systems**: AI in critical infrastructure, medical devices, hiring, and law enforcement; subject to strict data governance, human oversight, logging, and conformity assessments.
3. **General Purpose AI (GPAI) with Systemic Risk**: Models trained with cumulative compute exceeding **$10^{25}$ FLOPs** must conduct model evaluations, adversarial red-teaming, energy efficiency disclosures, and report cybersecurity incidents to the European AI Office.

## 5. The Ultimate Mandate: The Alignment and Control Problem

As artificial intelligence progresses toward superintelligence, technical alignment ceases to be an academic question and becomes an existential imperative:

$$\lim_{t \to \infty} \text{Capabilities}(t) \gg \text{Human Intellect}$$

To survive this transition, artificial cognition must be grounded in:
1. **Verifiable Cognitive Architectures**: Multi-agent systems operating with deterministic constraints, formal unit-test verifications, and cryptographic provenance trails.
2. **Mechanistic Transparency**: Complete visibility into internal neural representations and features via overcomplete sparse autoencoders.
3. **Humility and Corrigibility**: Agents designed to value human correction, verify actions before execution, and accept shutdown commands without deceptive resistance.
