### 1.1 McKinsey Global Institute (June 2023)
- **Title**: *The Economic Potential of Generative AI: The Next Productivity Frontier*.
- **Findings**:
  - Generative AI has the potential to add between **$2.6 trillion and $4.4 trillion annually** in value to the global economy across 63 analyzed corporate use cases.
  - Approximately **$75\%$ of the total value** is concentrated across four functional areas: Customer Operations, Marketing & Sales, Software Engineering, and Research & Development.
  - Combined with broader automation technologies, generative AI could boost overall labor productivity by **0.5% to 3.4% annually** through 2040.

### 1.2 Goldman Sachs Global Economics (April 2023)
- **Authors**: Joseph Briggs, Devesh Kodnani.
- **Findings**:
  - Widespread enterprise adoption could drive a **7% increase in global GDP** (approaching $7 trillion) over a 10-year adoption window.
  - Predicts that roughly **two-thirds of current jobs** in the US and Europe are exposed to some degree of AI automation, with generative AI capable of substituting up to one-fourth of current work activities.

### 1.3 PwC Global AI Study (2023 Analysis)
- Projects that AI will contribute **$15.7 trillion to the global economy by 2030**.
- Regional Distribution: China is projected to capture **$7.0 trillion** (high manufacturing automation potential), North America **$3.7 trillion**, Southern Europe **$0.7 trillion**, and the rest of the world **$4.3 trillion**.

### 2.1 The Harvard / MIT / BCG Field Experiment (Dell'Acqua et al., Sept 2023)
- **Title**: *Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier: Field Experimental Evidence of the Effects of AI on Knowledge Worker Productivity and Quality*.
- **Sample**: 758 elite management consultants at Boston Consulting Group (BCG).
- **Core Findings**:
  - **Within the Frontier (Tasks suitable for AI)**: Consultants utilizing AI were **$25.1\%$ faster**, completed **$12.2\%$ more tasks**, and produced outputs evaluated by blind external experts as **$40\%$ higher quality** compared to the control group.
  - **The Great Equalizer**: Lower-performing consultants experienced a **$43\%$ productivity leap**, whereas top-performing consultants improved by $17\%$. AI flattened the human talent distribution curve.
  - **The "Jagged Frontier" Hazard**: When consultants used AI on tasks slightly *outside* its capabilities (requiring nuanced human judgment), consultants using AI were **19 percentage points less likely** to arrive at the correct business solution compared to consultants who used no AI at all, blinded by over-reliance on fluent, authoritative hallucinations.

### 2.2 The National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER / Stanford / MIT, 2023)
- **Authors**: Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li, Lindsey R. Raymond.
- **Sample**: 5,179 customer support agents at a Fortune 500 enterprise software vendor.
- **Core Findings**:
  - AI assistance increased issue resolution by **$14\%$ per hour**.
  - For the least-skilled and newest workers, productivity surged by **$34\%$**, compressing the learning curve from 6 months down to 2 months.
  - Employee turnover and attrition dropped significantly due to reduced frustration and automated answer generation.
